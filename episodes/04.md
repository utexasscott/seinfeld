# Episode 4 — The Doll

**DRAW:** a doll that looks exactly like somebody's mother / an airport rental counter with no cars left / a close talker at a dinner party · eating a candy bar with a knife and fork / whether being right excuses how you said it / David Puddy, agreeing to something enormous without emotion

*(Venue, object, transgression and rule decks were nearly exhausted. Per-slot re-draws against the filtered list, dice not choice. The venue deck is down to one card and needs replenishing — see wrap.)*

**THEME:** Being right, and what it costs to say so out loud.

**A-PLOT (Kramer):** A doll off a stoop table that is, feature for feature, Estelle Costanza. It is the centerpiece of a dinner party in an apartment that isn't his.
**B-PLOT (George):** The muffin bottoms. Six weeks of decapitated muffins in the office kitchen, a memo, and a photograph. He is right and he intends to put it in writing.
**C-PLOT (Jerry):** One car. Four people who need it. He will not lend it, and he needs to be *right* about not lending it.
**D-PLOT (Elaine):** A 9:40 flight, a bag of muffin stumps she cannot get rid of, and nine years of never once being told she was right.

**COLLISION:** Estelle lands at 9:15. The doll is in the trunk. There are no cars left at the counter, and every single person in the terminal is correct.
