# Episode 1 — The Heat Lamp

**DRAW:** a rotisserie heat lamp visible through a window / a movie theater lobby, arguing about which showing / a low talker you keep agreeing with / whether an item left outside belongs to whoever takes it / Frank Costanza, arriving mid-argument about something unrelated

**THEME:** Possession. Everything unattended belongs to whoever gets there first — the light, the reservation, the salad, the girlfriend.

**A-PLOT (Jerry):** A rotisserie chicken place across the street aims its heat lamp into his bedroom. He goes to complain, can't hear a word the owner says, agrees to everything, and signs it.
**B-PLOT (Kramer):** He has one key to 5F, a dead man's mail, and a two-year-old reservation at Cavallino he believes he inherited.
**C-PLOT (Elaine):** A coworker took credit for her big salad and won't stop asking her out. She's declined three times, which is the legal maximum.
**D-PLOT (George):** A health club with one working shower, a badge he obtained by fraud, and a wheelchair whose brakes don't hold.

**COLLISION:** The reservation, the fake boyfriend, the stolen postcard and the heat lamp all arrive at Cavallino on Thursday at eight. Everybody wants the same table and nobody has a claim to anything.
