# Episode 2 — The Raincoat

**DRAW:** a raincoat being sold on consignment / a wedding nobody wants to attend / not saying hello back / how long you must wait before calling / Frank + Estelle Costanza, both, fighting, in the apartment

*(Redrawn for Burned collisions: B and C venues, B/C/D objects, D rule, guest hook.)*

**THEME:** What you owe. Nobody asked for the favor, and everybody's getting the bill.

**A-PLOT (Jerry):** His raincoat is on consignment at a shop he never took it to, and he's expected Saturday at the wedding of a man who did not say hello back.
**B-PLOT (Kramer):** He owes a funeral home a debt. The debt is a viewing running late for a man with no mourners, and Kramer is the room.
**C-PLOT (Elaine):** She praised a haircut that is a catastrophe. Now there is a jacket with a garish lining, and a car being held for her on the last day of the month.
**D-PLOT (George):** No shower, no suit, no invitation. A puffy shirt, and a line at a hospital he has a very plausible excuse for cutting.

**COLLISION:** The coat was sold to the funeral home — they buy coats to dress bodies. The man with no mourners is wearing it, and he is going into the ground in a plot Frank sold out from under Estelle in 1978. The wedding is four blocks away. Everyone ends the night in the wrong building, in the wrong coat, saying hello to nobody.
